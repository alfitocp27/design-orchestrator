Attic Black-Figure Amphora

Attributed to the Antimenes Painter • Bequest of Sir Arthur Evans Trust (1884)
MULTISPECTRAL REFLECTANCE (RAW 1:1)
Core Catalog Specifications
Culture & Period Archaic Greek (Attica, Athens) • c. 530–520 BCE
Classification / Media Terracotta ceramic with iron-rich black slip gloss
Physical Dimensions Height: 42.4 cm • Diameter (lip): 18.2 cm • Body: 28.1 cm
Current Location Gallery Room 4 (Classical Rotunda), Vitrine 12-B
Permanent Rack ID Vault 03 / Bay B / Rack 04 (Sub-shelf 2)
Legal Provenance Excavated Vulci (1842); Collection Prince Canino; Evans Bequest 1884
CURATORIAL RESEARCH & CONSERVATION RECORD

Side A depicts Herakles wrestling the Nemean Lion in the presence of Athena and Iolaos, framed by lotus-bud friezes on the neck. Side B features a Dionysiac procession with two satyrs and maenads. Micro-XRF analysis conducted in March 2024 confirms ancient lead clamp repairs on the lower handle junction dating to late 5th century BCE. No modern structural overpainting detected. The gloss remains exceptionally intact with minor mineral encrustation near the foot.
